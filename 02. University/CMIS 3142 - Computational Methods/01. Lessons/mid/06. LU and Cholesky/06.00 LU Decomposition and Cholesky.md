## Why this lesson matters
If you need to solve $Ax = b$ for **many different right-hand sides $b$** with the same $A$, it is wasteful to repeat Gaussian elimination each time. **LU decomposition** factors $A$ once into triangular matrices; each new $b$ then costs only two cheap triangular solves. **Cholesky** is the special, faster and more stable LU for **symmetric positive definite** matrices: $A = LL^T$.
