## Why this lesson matters
Differential equations (heat flow, fluid flow, growth of money) usually cannot be solved by formula. The **finite difference method** replaces each derivative by a **difference quotient** of function values at nearby **grid points**, so the differential equation becomes a system of **algebraic equations**, which the earlier lessons (GE, Cholesky, Jacobi, Gauss-Seidel, SOR) can solve. The lesson also covers **difference tables** and the **forward difference operator Δ**, used to tabulate and analyse sequences of values.
