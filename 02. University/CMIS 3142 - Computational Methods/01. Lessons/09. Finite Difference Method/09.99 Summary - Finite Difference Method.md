# 09.99 · Lesson Summary: Finite Difference Method

> [!info] Hub: [[09.00 Finite Difference Method]] · Past papers: [[PP 09 - Finite Difference Method]] · Priority 🔴 (3/4 papers)

## Must know
- Discretisation: grid of nodes; one algebraic equation per node; derivatives replaced by difference quotients from **Taylor series**.
- First derivative: forward $\frac{f_{i+1}-f_i}{\Delta x} + O(\Delta x)$, backward $\frac{f_i - f_{i-1}}{\Delta x} + O(\Delta x)$, central $\frac{f_{i+1}-f_{i-1}}{2\Delta x} + O(\Delta x^2)$, with derivations.
- Second derivative: central $\frac{f_{i+1}-2f_i+f_{i-1}}{\Delta x^2} + O(\Delta x^2)$; forward/backward versions $O(\Delta x)$.
- Mixed derivative (central/forward/backward) from the 2-variable Taylor series.
- Truncation error = neglected higher-order Taylor terms; round-off vs truncation; consistency, stability, convergence.
- Forward difference tables; constant $n$-th differences for degree-$n$ polynomials; missing terms; table algorithm/Java.
- Compound interest: $\Delta A_n = rA_n$, $A_{n+1} = (1+r)A_n$, $A_n = P(1+r)^n$; table and Java.
- Δ operator: $\Delta e^{ax}$, $\Delta^2e^x$, $\Delta\log x$, partial fractions, $\Delta^2(1/x)$.

## Important definitions
- **Finite difference:** a derivative replaced by an algebraic difference quotient.
- **Truncation error (FD):** difference between the exact solution of the differential equation and its finite difference solution without round-off; caused by dropping higher-order Taylor terms.
- **Order of accuracy:** power of $\Delta x$ in the leading error term.
- **Forward difference operator:** $\Delta f(x) = f(x+h) - f(x)$.

## Important formulas
$$
\left(\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x\partial y}\right)_{i,j} \approx \frac{f_{i+1,j+1} - f_{i-1,j+1} - f_{i+1,j-1} + f_{i-1,j-1}}{4\Delta x\Delta y}\ \ (\text{central}, O(\Delta x^2, \Delta y^2))
$$
$$
\text{forward: } \frac{f_{i+1,j+1} - f_{i,j+1} - f_{i+1,j} + f_{i,j}}{\Delta x\Delta y},\qquad \text{backward: } \frac{f_{i,j} - f_{i-1,j} - f_{i,j-1} + f_{i-1,j-1}}{\Delta x\Delta y}\ \ (O(\Delta x, \Delta y))
$$
$$
\Delta e^{ax} = e^{ax}(e^{ah}-1),\quad \Delta^2e^x = (e^h-1)^2e^x,\quad \Delta\log x = \log\!\left(1+\tfrac hx\right),\quad \Delta^2\tfrac1x\Big|_{h=1} = \tfrac{2}{x(x+1)(x+2)}
$$

## Important comparison
| Forward | Backward | Central |
|---|---|---|
| $x_i$, $x_{i+1}$ | $x_{i-1}$, $x_i$ | $x_{i-1}$, $x_{i+1}$ |
| $O(\Delta x)$ | $O(\Delta x)$ | $O(\Delta x^2)$ |
| use at left boundary | use at right boundary | interior points, most accurate |
