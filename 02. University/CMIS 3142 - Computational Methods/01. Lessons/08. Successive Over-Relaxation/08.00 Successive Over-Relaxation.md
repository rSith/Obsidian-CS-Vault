## Why this lesson matters
Gauss-Seidel often converges slowly. SOR takes the Gauss-Seidel update and **pushes it further** (over-relaxes) by a factor $\omega$, which can cut the number of iterations dramatically. It is one of the standard solvers for the large sparse systems produced by finite difference methods.
