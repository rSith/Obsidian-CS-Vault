## Why this lesson matters
Gaussian elimination (GE) is the basic **direct method** for solving a system of linear equations $Ax = b$: it gets the exact answer (apart from round-off) in a fixed number of steps. LU decomposition and Cholesky ([[06.00 LU Decomposition and Cholesky]]) are reorganised versions of it, and iterative methods ([[05.00 Iterative Methods - Jacobi and Gauss-Seidel]]) are its alternative for large sparse systems.
