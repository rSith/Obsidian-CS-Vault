## Why this lesson matters
Operational databases are built to **run the business** (record each sale, each registration). Managers also need to **understand the business** (sales trends over five years, by region and product). Running those big analytical queries on the operational database would be slow and would disturb daily work. A **data warehouse** is a separate, integrated, historical store designed for **analysis**, queried with **OLAP** tools.

> [!tip] Analogy
> The operational database is a shop's **till receipts drawer**: every transaction, right now. The data warehouse is the accountant's **annual report archive**: cleaned, combined from all branches, organised by year/product/region, and never edited, only added to.
