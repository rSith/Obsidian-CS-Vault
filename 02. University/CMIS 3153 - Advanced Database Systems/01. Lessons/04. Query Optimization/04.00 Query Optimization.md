## Why this lesson matters
The same query can be executed in many ways whose costs differ by **factors of thousands** (e.g. joining three tables via Cartesian products vs filtering first). The **query optimizer** chooses a good plan automatically; you, the user, never specify it. This lesson explains how.

> [!tip] Analogy (from the task brief)
> Query optimization is like **planning the fastest route on a map before driving**. You could drive through every town (Cartesian product) or take the highway and turn off only where needed (push selections down, join smartly). The optimizer studies the map (statistics) and the available roads (indexes, join algorithms) before you start.
