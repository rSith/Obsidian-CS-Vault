## Why this lesson matters
[[01.07 Heap and Sorted Files|Heap and sorted files]] are slow to search unless you search on the one field the file is sorted by. An **index** is a small, separate, sorted file of `(key value, pointer)` pairs that lets the DBMS jump to the right block in a handful of disk reads, without moving the data records.

> [!tip] Analogy for the whole lesson
> The index at the back of a textbook. You look up "B+ tree" in the alphabetical index, read "page 214", and go straight there instead of reading the whole book. A **multilevel index** is an index of the index (like the A–Z thumb tabs on a dictionary). A **B+-tree** is a multilevel index that **keeps itself balanced** as pages are added and removed.
