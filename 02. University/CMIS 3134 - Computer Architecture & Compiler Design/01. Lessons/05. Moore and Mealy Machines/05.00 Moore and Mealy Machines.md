## What this lesson is about

DFAs and NDFAs only answer **yes/no** (accept or reject). **Mealy** and **Moore** machines **produce output** as they run: one output symbol per step. They are **transducers** ([[03.08 Classification of Finite Automata]]), and they model real sequential circuits: vending machines, sequence detectors, traffic-light controllers.

The single fact that separates them:
- **Mealy:** output depends on the **present state AND the present input** (output is written on the **arrows**).
- **Moore:** output depends on the **present state only** (output is written **inside the states**).
