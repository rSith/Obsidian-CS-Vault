## 2023/24 · Q3(b) (2 marks)
> **Question:** List two (02) benefits and two (02) limitations of AI-Driven Web Development.

**Lesson:** [[14.01 AI for Automation and Efficiency]] · [[14.02 AI for User Experience and Design]] · [[14.05 Ethical Considerations and Limitations of AI]]

### Expected answer
**Benefits**
1. **Automation and speed:** AI tools such as GitHub Copilot and OpenAI Codex generate and complete code, automate testing (bug detection, performance) and generate content, which speeds up development.
2. **Personalised user experience:** AI analyses behaviour to personalise content, recommendations and navigation, and **chatbots** give 24/7 customer support.

**Limitations**
1. **Limited decision-making:** decisions are limited to the available data, and AI tools can't handle complex tasks independently; generated code must be checked manually.
2. **Biased training data** can lead to wrong or unethical decisions. *(Alternatives: large model sizes limit use on mobile devices; privacy and explainability concerns.)*

### How to approach this question
½ mark each, so give exactly **two of each**, one clear line each.
