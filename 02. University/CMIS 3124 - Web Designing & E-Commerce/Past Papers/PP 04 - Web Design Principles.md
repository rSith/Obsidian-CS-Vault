## 2023/24 · Q4(c) (4 marks)
> **Question:** Briefly explain the CARP design principles in Aesthetic Web Design.

**Lesson:** [[04.01 Aesthetic Layout and CARP Principles]]

### Expected answer
CARP guides the **layout** of a page. Layout forms relationships between elements, creates visual hierarchy and interest, and improves readability and usability.

- **Contrast:** two or more elements are made to look different in **colour, size, shape, texture, orientation, position or movement**, to group or separate them and to highlight the most important ones. *Example:* a brightly coloured "Buy now" button on a neutral page.
- **Alignment:** the **placement, position, orientation and grouping** of elements along common lines or a grid, so the page looks orderly and is easy to scan. *Example:* form labels and fields lined up.
- **Repetition:** repeating **colours, shapes, textures, fonts, typography, graphics and spatial relationships** across pages to create consistency and brand identity. *Example:* the same header and button style on every page.
- **Proximity:** using **white space and logical structure** (grouping related items close together) to create **visual unity**. *Example:* a product's image, price and "Add to cart" button grouped in one card.

One mark each: **definition (lecture wording) + a web example**.
