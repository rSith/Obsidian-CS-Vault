## 2023/24 · Q5(e) (5 marks)
> **Question:** "Social media integration and influencer marketing help to maximize the online presence and sales of brands". Discuss giving reasons.

**Lesson:** [[11.01 Social Media in E-Commerce]] · [[11.02 Benefits of Social Media Integration]] · [[11.03 Influencer Marketing]]

### Expected answer
I agree. Social media is **visual, interactive and real-time**, thrives on **user-generated content** and lets brands **micro-target** consumers by behaviour, preferences and interests. Through **social commerce**, users can even buy inside the platform (Instagram Shopping, Facebook live-shopping events).

**Online presence:** social media integration enhances **brand awareness** (sharing, viral content), builds **community and loyalty**, supports **customer engagement** through comments and live Q&A, **drives traffic** to the e-commerce site, and the extra visibility supports SEO.

**Sales:** **targeted advertising and lead generation** reach likely buyers; **social proof** (reviews, likes, UGC) builds trust; **real-time analytics** let brands optimise campaigns quickly.

**Influencer marketing:** influencers have **established credibility**, so followers feel **psychological trust** in their recommendations. They create **urgency and exclusivity** with limited-time codes and "special access" deals. Strategies include **sponsored posts, affiliate links/discount codes, product reviews and unboxing videos, and live streams**. **Micro and nano influencers** give especially high engagement and conversion in niche markets.

**Balance:** brands must manage **fake followers, disclosure/transparency rules and market saturation**. Used well, social media and influencers clearly maximise presence and sales.

## 2023/24 · Q6(a) (4 marks)
> **Question:** What is Search Engine Optimization (SEO)? Give two (02) on-page SEO techniques.

**Lesson:** [[11.05 How Search Engines Work]] · [[11.06 On-Page SEO]]

### Expected answer
**SEO** is the practice of **improving a website's content and structure so that search engines can crawl, index and rank it higher in organic (unpaid) search results**, increasing its visibility and bringing more relevant visitors. (Search engines crawl, render, index and rank pages.)

**On-page techniques (any two, explained):**
1. **Unique, accurate page titles and a meta description:** the `<title>` tells users and search engines the page's topic and is shown in results; the meta description summarises the page (the snippet).
2. **Simple, readable URLs and a shallow directory structure, with breadcrumbs:** convey the content clearly in results and help navigation.
3. *(Alternatives)* **Image alt text and descriptive file names**; **keyword-rich anchor text, heading tags and relevant content keywords**.

## 2019/20 · Q5(b)(iii) (5 marks)
> **Question:** You decided to sell religious (Hindu, Buddhism and Christianity) artifacts online through a website. … iii. How do you promote this website among potential customers?

**Lesson:** [[11.08 Content Marketing]] · [[11.06 On-Page SEO]] · [[11.07 Off-Page SEO]] · [[11.03 Influencer Marketing]]

### Expected answer
1. **On-page SEO:** keyword-rich titles, meta descriptions, headings and alt text (e.g. "brass Buddha statue", "Hindu pooja items", "Christian rosary"); simple URLs.
2. **Off-page SEO:** backlinks from cultural, temple and church sites and directories; submit an XML sitemap.
3. **Social media:** pages and groups, plus **targeted ads** micro-targeted by religion-related interests and timed for **festivals** (Vesak, Deepavali, Christmas).
4. **Influencers:** micro/nano influencers in cultural niches; reviews and unboxing videos; affiliate discount codes.
5. **Content marketing:** blog posts and videos on the meaning and care of artefacts, email newsletters, seasonal promotions; display **customer reviews** as social proof.

## 2017/18 · Q8(c)
> **Question:** Suppose you published several blogs. But you could not get expected number of comments. What are the possible ways to make the publicity to your blogs?

**Lesson:** [[11.08 Content Marketing]] · [[11.06 On-Page SEO]] · [[11.07 Off-Page SEO]] · [[01.07 RSS Feeds]] · [[01.08 Social Bookmarking]]

### Expected answer
1. **Improve SEO:** clear, keyword-rich titles, meta descriptions, headings and alt text; simple URLs; submit a sitemap so search engines find the posts.
2. **Share on social media** and in online communities; add **social sharing buttons** so readers spread posts.
3. Use **social bookmarking** and offer an **RSS "subscribe" feed** so readers are notified of new posts.
4. **Build backlinks:** guest posts and thoughtful comments on related blogs (links drive PageRank).
5. **Content marketing:** post **consistently** on engaging topics; **end posts with questions** to invite comments; **reply to every comment**; email newsletter.
6. **Collaborate** with other bloggers or influencers.
