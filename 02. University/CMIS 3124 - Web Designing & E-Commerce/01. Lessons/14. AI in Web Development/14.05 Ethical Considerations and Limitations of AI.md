# 14.05 · Ethical Considerations and Limitations of AI in Web Development

> [!info] [[14.00 AI in Web Development|Lesson 14]] · Lecture ref L14.6–L14.7 · Priority 🔴 High
> **Asked as:** "List two (02) benefits and two (02) limitations of AI-Driven Web Development." (2023/24 Q3(b), 2 marks)

## Part A: Ethical considerations (lecture)

| Issue | Lecture explanation | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Data privacy and security** | AI **depends heavily on data** to work | **Be transparent** about data collection ([[10.10 GDPR]]) |
| **Bias in AI algorithms** | AI can only perform based on its **training data**; if the data contains **biases**, its judgements **mirror those biases** | Use tools such as **IBM's AI Fairness 360** to **detect and correct bias** |
| **Transparency and explainability** | AI algorithms are **poor at explaining their reasoning**; professionals can't fully understand them because of their **complexity and abstract nature** | Prefer explainable models; document decisions |
| **Accountability and responsibility** | When AI makes mistakes, it is **hard to decide who is responsible** for the consequences | **AI should not function independently** without **oversight and validation** by developers and businesses |
| **Environmental impact** | **Large language models and deep learning** consume **large amounts of resources** (energy) | Build **energy-efficient** AI models and infrastructure |
| **Job displacement and economic impact** | AI may replace some jobs | Use AI **to enhance human skills**; **invest in training** so employees move to roles that need expertise |
| **Ethical use of generative AI** | AI-generated content can **spread false information**; **deepfakes** and manipulation create **legal challenges** | Label AI content; verify facts |

**Deepfake** = realistic fake video, image or audio of a real person created by AI.

## Part B: Limitations of AI and ML in web development (lecture)
| Limitation | Lecture explanation |
|---|---|
| **Limited decision-making capability** | ML decisions are **limited to the data available** to them; AI development tools **can't deal with complex tasks independently** |
| **Risk of biased data** | **Biased training data** leads AI to make **wrong or unethical decisions** |
| **Model size limitations** | **Large models** can't easily run on **customers' mobile devices** |

## Part C: Model answer for 2023/24 Q3(b) (2 marks)
**Benefits (any two):**
1. **Automates coding, testing and content generation** (e.g. GitHub Copilot, Codex), which speeds up development.
2. **Personalised UX**: tailored content, recommendations and adaptive interfaces; **24/7 chatbots**.
*(Also: performance optimisation and threat detection; automated design suggestions.)*

**Limitations (any two):**
1. **Limited decision-making**: only as good as its data; can't handle complex tasks alone; output (e.g. Codex code) **must be checked manually**.
2. **Biased training data** leads to wrong or unethical decisions.
*(Also: large model size limits use on mobile devices; privacy and explainability concerns.)*

½ mark each: give exactly two of each, one line each.
