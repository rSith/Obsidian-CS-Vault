# 14.99 · Lesson Summary: The Role of AI in Web Development

> [!abstract] Back to [[14.00 AI in Web Development]] · Practice: [[PP 14 - AI in Web Development]]

## Must know
- **Automation and efficiency:** code generation and analysis (**Codex**: output must be checked manually; **GitHub Copilot** completes code), automated testing (bugs, performance), content generation, workflow optimisation.
- **Enhanced UX:** personalised experiences, personalised recommendations, improved navigation, **24/7 chatbots**.
- **Performance and security:** find slow loading and inefficient code; detect malware and hacking attempts.
- **Design:** automated design suggestions; **adaptive interfaces** in real time.
- **8 trends:** AI personalisation (dynamic content, adaptive interfaces, predictive analytics) · voice search (NLP, schema markup, local SEO) · AI accessibility (alt text, screen readers, WCAG monitoring) · generative AI (ChatGPT, DALL·E, Jasper) · AI cybersecurity (real-time threats, fraud, encryption) · PWAs + AI (offline caching, performance, push) · AI e-commerce (visual search, chatbots, inventory) · low-code/no-code.
- **Ethics (7):** privacy and security · bias (**IBM AI Fairness 360**) · transparency and explainability · accountability (human oversight) · environmental impact · job displacement · generative-AI misuse (false information, deepfakes).
- **Limitations (3):** limited decision-making · biased data · model size (mobile).

## Exam focus
- 🔴 Two benefits + two limitations (2)
- 🟡 Trends feed the security (Q3(e)) and PWA questions
