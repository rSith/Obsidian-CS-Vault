# 14.02 · Role of AI: Enhanced User Experience and Design

> [!info] [[14.00 AI in Web Development|Lesson 14]] · Lecture ref L14.2, L14.4 · Priority 🟡 Medium

## Part A: Enhanced user experience (lecture)
| Use | Lecture explanation |
|---|---|
| **Personalised experiences** | AI **analyses user behaviour** to **personalise content, recommendations and website design**, increasing **engagement and satisfaction** |
| **Personalised content recommendations** | Tailored **product recommendations, content suggestions and search results** ([[10.08 Recommendation Engines]]) |
| **Improved website navigation** | AI **optimises navigation based on user behaviour**, so users find what they need more easily ([[05.07 Navigation Design]]) |
| **Chatbots for customer support** | **24/7 support**, answering questions and resolving issues **quickly and efficiently** |

## Part B: AI in design (lecture)
| Use | Lecture explanation |
|---|---|
| **Automated design suggestions** | AI **generates design suggestions** based on **user preferences and website content**, making visually appealing sites easier to create |
| **Adaptive interfaces** | **Dynamic interfaces that adapt to user behaviour and preferences in real time** |

**Adaptive interface** example: a news site moves the "Sports" section to the top for a user who always reads sports, and switches to a larger font for a user who keeps zooming in.

## Benefits summary (useful for 2023/24 Q3(b))
- Higher **engagement and satisfaction** through personalisation.
- **24/7 support** at low cost.
- **Easier navigation** and faster design.
