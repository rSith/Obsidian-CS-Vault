## What this lesson is about
How **AI changes the way websites are built and experienced**:
1. **four roles**: automation and efficiency, enhanced UX, performance and security, design;
2. **eight emerging AI trends**;
3. **ethical considerations**;
4. **limitations** of AI and ML in web development.
