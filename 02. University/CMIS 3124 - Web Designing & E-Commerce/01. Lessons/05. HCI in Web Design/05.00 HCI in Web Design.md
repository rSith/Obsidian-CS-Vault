## What this lesson is about
**HCI** is the study of how people interact with computers. This lesson applies it to websites: understanding users, the **difference between UI and UX**, designing layout, controls, appearance and navigation, **17 UI design principles**, **cognitive load theory**, and **usability testing methods**.

## What is design? (lecture)
- In web design: *"the process of **planning and arranging content** on a website, focusing on the **visual aspects and user experience (UX)** to create an attractive, informative and engaging online presence."*
- In general: **"achieving goals within constraints."**
  - **Goals** = purpose: *who is it for, why do they want it?*
  - **Constraints** = materials, platforms.

## Three questions every WebApp interface must answer (lecture)
| Question | What the interface must do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Where am I?** | Indicate **which WebApp** has been accessed, and the user's **location in the content hierarchy** | Logo, page title, breadcrumbs |
| **What can I do?** | Help the user understand **current options**: which functions are available, which links are live, what content is relevant | Highlighted buttons, active menu items |
| **Where have I been, where am I going?** | **Facilitate navigation**: provide a **"map"** of where the user has been and what paths lead elsewhere | Visited-link colour, progress steps "1 › 2 › 3" |
