# 05.02 · HCI: Definition, Goals and Factors

> [!info] [[05.00 HCI in Web Design|Lesson 5]] · Lecture ref L5.5 · Priority 🟢 Low (not asked directly)

## 1. What is HCI?
**Human–Computer Interaction (HCI)** is **"a multidisciplinary field that studies and designs how people interact with computer systems and technologies, aiming to create user-friendly, efficient and enjoyable experiences."**
- It **focuses on the interaction between humans (users) and computers**.
- **UIs are designed based on fundamental principles of HCI.**
- Lesson 4 gives another definition: *"the study, planning, design and uses of the interaction between people and computers"* ([[04.04 Functional and Usable Design]]).

## 2. Goals of HCI (lecture)
**Safety · Effectiveness · Efficiency · Usability · Appeal**

| Goal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Safety | The system protects users from harm and serious errors |
| Effectiveness | Users can actually achieve their goals |
| Efficiency | Users achieve them quickly, with little effort |
| Usability | Easy to learn and use ([[04.04 Functional and Usable Design\|LEMES]]) |
| Appeal | Pleasant and attractive to use |

## 3. HCI factors (lecture)

| Factor | Examples (lecture) |
|---|---|
| **Organisational** | Job roles, work organisation, lighting, health and safety |
| **Users** | Cognitive processes and capabilities, motivation, enjoyment, satisfaction, personality, experience, comfort, seating, equipment, layout |
| **User interface** | Input devices, output devices, dialogue structures, use of colour, icons, commands, navigation, graphics, natural language, user support, multimedia |
| **Task** | Easy, complex, novel; task allocation, monitoring, skills |
| **Constraints** | Cost, timescales, budgets, staff, equipment, buildings |
| **System functionality** | Hardware, software, application |
| **Productivity** | Increase output, increase quality, decrease costs, decrease errors, increase innovation |

> [!tip] Mnemonic
> **"Our Users Use Tasks; Constraints Shape Productivity"**: Organisational, Users, UI, Task, Constraints, System functionality, Productivity.
