# 01.10 · Web 4.0 and the Future of the Internet

> [!info] [[01.00 Evolution of the Web|Lesson 1]] · Lecture ref L1.8–L1.9 · Priority 🔴 High, as the last paragraphs of the evolution essay (2023/24 Q1(d)).

## Part A: Web 4.0 (the symbiotic web)

### 1. What is it?
**Web 4.0** is **"the Symbiotic Web"** and **the next stage in Internet evolution**. In it, **artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning and connected devices converge** to create **a seamless and highly intuitive digital ecosystem**.

*Symbiotic* means that humans and machines **live and work together for mutual benefit**.

- It focuses on **interconnecting humans, machines and systems**, which enables **smarter, more autonomous interactions** and **hyper-personalised experiences**.
- It aims to make the web **predictive, context-aware and capable of real-time decision-making**, *"ultimately blurring the line between physical and digital realities."*

### 2. Features (lecture list)
1. **Tailored experiences** based on user behaviour and preferences (hyper-personalisation).
2. **Devices, platforms and systems work seamlessly together**.
3. **Enhanced immersive experiences** in education, retail and entertainment.
4. **AI-driven applications continuously learn and improve** from user interactions.
5. **Voice, gesture and brain-computer interfaces (BCI)** for intuitive control.
6. **Real-time decision-making** by smart devices and bots.

### 3. Applications (lecture list)

| Area | Web 4.0 application |
|---|---|
| **Smart cities** | IoT devices, sensors and AI optimise **energy use, traffic management and public services** |
| **Smart homes** | Connected devices work together for efficient, sustainable living |
| **Healthcare** | AI + **wearables** → **real-time health monitoring, predictive diagnostics, personalised treatment** |
| **Education & entertainment** | **AR/VR** immersive online classrooms and interactive gaming |
| **Finance & supply chain** | Secure, transparent transactions and **decentralised services** |
| **Transport** | Predictive systems enable **self-driving vehicles and smart logistics** |

## Part B: Future of the Internet (7 directions from the lecture)

| # | Direction | Plain-English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hyper-personalisation** | Content and services tailored to each person |
| 2 | **Integration of IoT and ubiquitous computing** | Everyday objects connected; computing happens everywhere |
| 3 | **Intelligent and emotional web** | Systems that understand context and even emotion |
| 4 | **Decentralisation at scale** | Blockchain-based, user-owned services for everyone |
| 5 | **Immersive experiences with AR/VR and the metaverse** | 3-D, virtual and mixed-reality spaces |

## Part C: Answering the evolution essay (2023/24 Q1(d), 8 marks)

*"Describe how the nature and structure of the Web, as well as the way we use it, have been continuously evolving."*

Write **one short paragraph per era** (about 1.5 marks each), then a closing line on the trend:
1. **Web 1.0:** static, read-only portals; few images; AltaVista.
2. **Web 2.0:** participatory read-write web; UGC; blogs, wikis, social media; Gmail/YouTube; APIs, cloud.
3. **Web 2.5:** hybrid; blockchain, NFTs, privacy on top of centralised platforms.
4. **Web 3.0:** decentralised, P2P, user-owned data; encryption, smart contracts, DeFi.
5. **Web 4.0:** symbiotic; AI + ML + IoT; predictive, context-aware, real-time; voice/gesture/BCI.
6. **Trend / future:** read → write → own → interact with intelligent systems; hyper-personalisation, IoT, AR/VR, quantum internet, sustainability.
