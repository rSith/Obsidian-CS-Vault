# 01.08 · Social Bookmarking

> [!info] [[01.00 Evolution of the Web|Lesson 1]] · Lecture ref L1.5(e) · Priority 🔴 High
> **Asked as:** short note (2019/20 Q6(b)(v)); "describe the advantages of social bookmarking" (2018/19 Q5(a)(ii)); choosing a tool to share a journal paper (2019/20 Q6(a)(iii)).

## 1. What is it?

**Social bookmarking** is **"a browser-based function that allows us to save, sort and share webpage links digitally."**

A normal browser bookmark is saved only on your own computer. A **social** bookmark is saved **online, in your account**, where you can **organise** it and **share** it with others.

## 2. How do you access it?
- Through a **dedicated bookmarking platform**, or
- Through an **extension added to your web browser**.

You save bookmarked sources to your account, where you can create **sorting folders, categories or tags** so that sources are easy to organise and find.

## 3. Key properties (lecture)

| Property | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Web-based** | Bookmarks are **saved outside the device's storage**, so you can reach them **from anywhere, on any device** with an Internet connection |
| **Real-time updates** | Social bookmarks update in real time as new sources are saved |
| **Trend tracking** | The service tracks **how many bookmarks a source or tag receives** to identify **trends** |
| **Visibility** | These trends can **boost social-site traffic** and **increase visibility on search engine results pages (SERPs)** |

## 4. How does it work? (lecture)
1. **Algorithms identify patterns and trends** in bookmarking and form **categories** of related content.
2. The **social aspect** includes sharing sources, **re-posting** others' bookmarks, **commenting** on and responding to posts, and **following users or tags**.
3. Some features make these social functions available **from other websites**, which encourages collaboration.
4. The algorithm **suggests tags or users to follow** based on what you bookmarked before.

## 5. Advantages (answer to 2018/19 Q5(a)(ii))
1. **Access anywhere, any device:** bookmarks are stored online, not on one computer.
2. **Organisation:** tags, folders and categories make sources easy to find later.
3. **Sharing and discovery:** share links with others and find new sources through other users' bookmarks, comments and follows.
4. **Real-time trends:** see what is popular. Algorithms suggest relevant tags and users.
5. **For site owners:** more **traffic** and better **SERP visibility**.

## 6. When to choose it (scenario questions)
- An **accepted journal paper** or a **scientific article** that you want other researchers to find and build on → **social bookmarking**, ideally with RSS. Researchers can save, tag, share and follow it, and trends increase its visibility.

## 7. Exam-relevant points
- Definition: **"save, sort and share webpage links digitally"**, **browser-based**.
- **Web-based storage → any device**; **tags/folders**; **trends → SERP visibility**; social features (re-post, comment, follow).
- The lecture also asks "What are the ways to use social bookmarking?" as a discussion slide. Use the advantages above to answer it.
