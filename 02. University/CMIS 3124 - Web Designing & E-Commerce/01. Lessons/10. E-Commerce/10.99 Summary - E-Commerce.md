# 10.99 · Lesson Summary: E-Commerce

> [!abstract] Back to [[10.00 E-Commerce]] · Practice: [[PP 10 - E-Commerce]]

## Must know
- **Definition:** the use of electronic communications and digital information-processing technology in business transactions to create, transform and redefine relationships for value creation between organisations, and between organisations and individuals. Any transaction completed solely through electronic means.
- **Timeline:** CompuServe/EDI 1969 → Aldrich 1979 → BCE 1982 → Electronic Mall 1984 → first secure transaction 1994 (Sting CD, NetMarket) → Amazon and eBay 1995 → PayPal 1998 → dot-com bust + AdWords/PPC 2000 → Cyber Monday + Prime 2005 → Shopify 2006 → Instacart 2012 → $29.267T in 2017 → **COVID-19 boost 2020** (about 5 years' acceleration).
- **Traditional vs e-commerce:** cost-effective, time-saving, convenient, easy expansion, quick product feedback, more sales, 24×7, automated, end-to-end **vs** no inspection, not for perishables/high-value goods, needs technical staff, screen-to-face, cyber fraud.
- **E-business** = beyond buying and selling (SCM, order processing, CRM, ERP, BI, collaboration); no money transaction needed.
- **6 framework layers:** application services → information brokerage and management → interface and support → secure messaging → middleware → network infrastructure.
- **Types:** B2B, B2C, C2C, C2B. **e-Gov:** G2C (tax returns), G2B (procurement, registrations), G2G (LGN, eSLIMS, NSDI, eBMD, e-GP).
- **Platforms:** open-source / SaaS / headless (CaaS, cart decoupled, APIs). **Features:** storefront, content tools, acquisition and loyalty, analytics, shipping and fulfilment, privacy and security (gateways, SSL, fraud detection), back-end integration.
- **Shopping cart:** add → manage → checkout → payment → order management (+ marketing). **Good cart:** user-friendly, mobile-friendly, secure, integrated, scalable.
- **Authentication:** verify identity before access. **Why:** security, fraud prevention, trust, non-repudiation. **Methods:** password, 2FA, MFA, biometrics, OTP, digital certificates, keystroke dynamics.
- **Recommendation engines:** collaborative (user-based, item-based: Netflix), content-based, demographic, community-based, hybrid.
- **Ethics:** why (trust, compliance, brand, sustainability); key areas (transparency, fairness, privacy, consumer rights, sustainability, IP, ethical marketing); **7 principles** (Golden Rule, Universalism, Slippery Slope, Collective Utilitarian, Risk Aversion, Perfect Information, Social Contract).
- **GDPR:** EU, applies worldwide to EU data; **7 principles**; accountability; security (72-hour breach notice); by design; 6 lawful bases; consent rules (under 13 needs a parent); **8 rights**.
- **PCI:** DSS, PTS, PA-DSS, P2PE; DSS = **6 goals, 12 requirements**.
- **8 consumer rights:** basic needs, safety, informed, choose, heard, **redress**, education, healthy environment.

## Important comparisons
| E-commerce | E-business |
|---|---|
| Buying and selling online | All electronic business processes |
| Money transaction | Money not necessary |

| 2FA | MFA |
|---|---|
| Exactly 2 factors | 2 or more factors (know / have / are) |

| Collaborative filtering | Content-based filtering |
|---|---|
| Similar users' behaviour | Similar item attributes |
