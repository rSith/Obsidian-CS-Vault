## What this lesson is about
**E-commerce** is buying, selling and building business relationships **through electronic means**. The lecture covers:
1. the **definition** and **history** of e-commerce;
2. **traditional commerce vs e-commerce**; **e-business**; the **six-layer architectural framework**;
3. **types** (B2B, B2C, C2C, C2B) and **e-government** (G2C, G2B, G2G);
4. **platforms**, **shopping carts**, **authentication**, **recommendation engines**;
5. **ethics and policies**: key ethical areas, **ethical principles**, **GDPR**, **PCI standards**, **consumer rights**.
