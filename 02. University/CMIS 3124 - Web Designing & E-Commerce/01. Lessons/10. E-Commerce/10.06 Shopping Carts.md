# 10.06 · Shopping Carts

> [!info] [[10.00 E-Commerce|Lesson 10]] · Lecture ref L10.9 · Priority 🔴 High
> **Asked as:** "Describe clearly the use of shopping cart available in e-commerce websites" (2018/19 Q3(a)); "advantages of an online shopping cart compared to an actual shopping cart" (2019/20 Q3(c), 8 marks).

## 1. What is a shopping cart?
An online **shopping cart** is the part of an e-commerce website that **holds the products a customer has selected** while they continue browsing, like a physical trolley in a supermarket, and then takes them through **checkout and payment**.

Technically, the cart remembers its items between page views using **sessions and cookies**, because HTTP is stateless ([[02.05 Sessions and Cookies]]).

## 2. Functionality (lecture)
```text
Adding items → Managing items → Proceeding to checkout → Payment processing → Order management
                                   (+ Marketing tools throughout)
```
| Function | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Adding items** | Put a product in the cart from its page |
| **Managing items** | Change quantity, remove, save for later; see a running total |
| **Proceeding to checkout** | Enter delivery address and choose a shipping option |
| **Payment processing** | Pay securely through a payment gateway |
| **Order management** | Confirmation, order number, tracking, history |
| **Marketing tools** | Discount codes, upselling ("you may also like"), abandoned-cart reminders |

> [!note]
> The lecture lists the six function names. The "what it does" column is explanation from the study guide.

## 3. Key features of a good shopping cart (lecture)
| Feature | Lecture explanation |
|---|---|
| **User-friendly interface** | Easy to navigate and understand |
| **Mobile-friendly design** | Optimised for viewing and use on various devices |
| **Secure payment processing** | Protects card data (HTTPS, PCI DSS: [[10.11 PCI DSS and Consumer Rights]]) |
| **Integration with other systems** | Integrates with other e-commerce platforms, **payment gateways and shipping providers** |
| **Scalability** | Handles **increasing traffic and orders** as the business grows |

## 4. Online cart vs physical cart (2019/20 Q3(c))
| Online shopping cart advantage | Why it is better than a physical trolley |
|---|---|
| **Available 24×7 from anywhere** | No travel, queues or carrying heavy items |
| **Automatic totals** | Calculates subtotals, taxes, discounts and delivery instantly |
| **Instant stock information** | Shows availability before you go to checkout |
| **Persistence** | Items are **saved for later** across sessions and devices |
| **Easy editing** | Add, remove and change quantities with a click |
| **Recommendations** | Related products and personalised suggestions |
| **Comparison and reviews** | Easy price comparison and reviews on each product |
| **Integrated payment and tracking** | Secure payment, digital receipts, order tracking |
| **Unlimited capacity** | Never "full" |
| **For the business** | Data on abandoned carts and buying patterns for marketing |
