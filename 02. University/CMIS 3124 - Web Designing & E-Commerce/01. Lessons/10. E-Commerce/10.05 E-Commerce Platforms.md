# 10.05 · E-Commerce Platforms (and Features of a Good E-Commerce Website)

> [!info] [[10.00 E-Commerce|Lesson 10]] · Lecture ref L10.8 · Priority 🔴 High
> **Asked as:** "Describe features of a good e-commerce website" (2017/18 Q2(b), 2018/19 Q4(b)); platform choice when planning a site (2017/18 Q2(a), 2018/19 Q4(a)); details to include on a religious-artefact store (2019/20 Q5(b)(i)).

## 1. What is an e-commerce platform? (lecture)
- The **content management system (CMS) and commerce engine** that websites use to **manage catalogued products, register purchases and manage a user's relationship** with an online retailer.
- **A software solution that lets businesses centrally manage their virtual assets, omnichannel marketing, digital sales and operations.**
- Many companies have switched to **digital experience platforms (DXP)** or **agile CMSs** to centralise marketing and sales.

*Omnichannel* means selling across many channels (website, app, social media, physical store) in one connected way.

## 2. Types of e-commerce platforms (lecture)

| Type | Characteristics | Best for | Example* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Open-source** | You can **modify and control everything** | **Tech-heavy companies with internal DevOps teams** | WooCommerce, Magento Open Source |
| **SaaS** (Software as a Service) | **Rented from a provider**; **no need to build or maintain** software; quick to get running | Businesses that want to **launch quickly** | Shopify, BigCommerce |
| **Headless commerce** | Content as a service (**CaaS**); **decouples the shopping cart from the CMS** completely; platform flexibility, open to future **front-end changes**; **APIs** connect a separate SaaS cart | Companies that want custom front-ends (apps, kiosks, PWAs) | Commercetools |

\* *Examples per type are illustrations. The lecture lists examples together: BigCommerce, Shopify, Adobe Commerce Cloud, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, WooCommerce, Commercetools.*

**"Headless"** means the **"head" (front-end presentation)** is separated from the **"body" (back-end commerce engine)**. They communicate through **APIs**, so the same back-end can serve a website, a mobile app and a smart-TV app.

## 3. Features of an e-commerce platform (lecture)

| Feature | What it provides |
|---|---|
| **Storefront technology** | Templates with **easy navigation, clear product displays, efficient checkout, responsive design** for mobile |
| **Content management tools** | Customise product pages, highlight features, craft marketing messages |
| **Customer acquisition and loyalty tools** | **Unified customer profiles** for personalisation; **social-channel integration** |
| **Data and analytics** | Reports on **traffic, sales, customer interactions**; shopper behaviour, **search trends, order history** |
| **Shipping and fulfilment** | Post-purchase events: **inventory, order routing, status monitoring, returns management** |
| **Privacy and security** | **Secure payment gateways, SSL certificates, fraud detection** |
| **Integration with back-end systems** | **Payment processing, order management, customer service platforms**, for efficiency and consistency |

## 4. Features of a good e-commerce website (answer to the past question)
Combine the platform features with other lectures:
1. **Usable storefront:** easy navigation, categories, **search and filters**, clear product images and details, **responsive** mobile-friendly design, fast loading ([[04.04 Functional and Usable Design]], [[06.00 Responsive Web Design]]).
2. **A good shopping cart and checkout:** add and manage items, guest checkout, clear total costs, multiple payment options ([[10.06 Shopping Carts]]).
3. **Security and trust:** HTTPS/SSL, a secure payment gateway, fraud detection, strong authentication, privacy policy (GDPR, PCI DSS) ([[10.07 Authentication in E-Commerce]]).
4. **Personalisation:** recommendation engines, wish lists, customer accounts and order history ([[10.08 Recommendation Engines]]).
5. **Social proof:** reviews and ratings; social media integration ([[11.02 Benefits of Social Media Integration]]).
6. **Fulfilment and support:** shipping options and tracking, a clear **return/refund policy**, contact/chat support; analytics for the owner.
7. **Accessibility:** usable by people with disabilities ([[09.00 Web Accessibility]]).
