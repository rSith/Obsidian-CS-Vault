## What this lesson is about
**Web accessibility** means that **people with disabilities can perceive, understand, navigate and use the web**. **Inclusive design** means designing for the full range of human diversity from the start.

The lesson explains:
1. **why** accessibility matters;
2. the **components** (content, user agents, authoring tools) and the **W3C guidelines** (WCAG, UAAG, ATAG);
3. **practical techniques** (alt text, captions, contrast, keyboard, accessible PDFs…);
4. **ARIA** (Accessible Rich Internet Applications): why, the **five rules**, **roles/properties/states**, **when to use it**, and how to make a **tab widget** accessible step by step.
