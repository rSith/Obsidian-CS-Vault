## What this lesson is about
How businesses use **social media, influencers and search engines** to get noticed and sell:
1. social media in e-commerce and **social commerce**;
2. **benefits** of social-media integration;
3. **influencer marketing** (tiers, strategies, why it works, challenges);
4. integrating **social-media APIs** into a web app;
5. **how search engines work**; **on-page** and **off-page SEO**;
6. **content marketing**.
