# 11.02 · Benefits of Social Media Integration

> [!info] [[11.00 Social Media and Web Influence|Lesson 11]] · Lecture ref L11.2 · Priority 🔴 High (2023/24 Q5(e); "prove you sell reliable items" uses social proof)

## The eight benefits (lecture list + explanation)

| Benefit | Explanation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Enhanced brand awareness** | Content reaches and is **shared** by huge audiences; it can go viral | A recipe video shared 10,000 times shows the brand's cookware |
| **Targeted advertising and lead generation** | **Micro-targeting** by demographics, behaviour and interests; captures potential customers ("leads") | An ad shown only to new parents in Colombo |
| **Social proof and trust building** | **Reviews, likes, shares, UGC and endorsements** persuade others: "if others trust it, so can I" | 4.8★ from 2,000 buyers |
| **Customer engagement and relationship building** | Comments, direct messages, live Q&A, polls | Replying to a customer's question within minutes |
| **Increased traffic to e-commerce sites** | Links, shoppable posts, swipe-ups lead to the store | An Instagram post links straight to the product page |
| **Influencer marketing** | Borrow the **credibility** of influencers ([[11.03 Influencer Marketing]]) | A micro-influencer's honest review |
| **Access to real-time analytics** | Measure reach, engagement, clicks and conversions immediately, and adjust campaigns | Stop an ad that isn't working after one day |
| **Building community and loyalty** | Groups and followers who return and recommend | A brand's Facebook group of loyal customers |

> [!note]
> The lecture lists the eight benefit names. The explanations and examples come from the study guide.

**Social proof** is the psychological tendency to follow what other people do. In e-commerce: reviews, ratings, "1,200 bought this week", user photos.

## Exam use
- "Social media integration … maximise online presence and sales" (2023/24 Q5(e)): use these benefits for **presence** (awareness, engagement, traffic, community, SEO support) and **sales** (targeted ads, social proof, analytics, influencers).
- "How can you prove you are selling reliable items?" (eBay question): **social proof** (seller ratings and reviews).
