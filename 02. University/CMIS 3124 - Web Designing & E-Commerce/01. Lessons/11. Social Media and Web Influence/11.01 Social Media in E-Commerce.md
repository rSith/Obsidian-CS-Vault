# 11.01 · Social Media in E-Commerce and Social Commerce

> [!info] [[11.00 Social Media and Web Influence|Lesson 11]] · Lecture ref L11.1 · Priority 🔴 High (part of 2023/24 Q5(e), 5 marks; the platform choices in 2019/20 Q6(a))

## 1. Why social media suits e-commerce (lecture)
- Social media is **inherently visual and interactive**.
- It **thrives on user-generated content (UGC)**: posts, reviews, photos and videos created by users, not by the brand.
- Platforms have evolved from **simple social networks into sophisticated marketing ecosystems**:
  - they **share content in real time**;
  - they **micro-target** specific consumer demographics based on **behaviour, preferences and interests**;
  - they offer **advanced analytics and advertising tools**.

**Micro-targeting** means showing an ad only to a very specific group (e.g. "women aged 20–30 in Kandy interested in fitness").

## 2. Platforms (lecture)

| Platform | How it is used for e-commerce |
|---|---|
| **Facebook** | Began as a way of **connecting people**; brands now use **paid ads and promotions**; deals promoted through **videos, live streams and stories**; brands collaborate with influencers on **live shopping events**, where users **buy directly during the stream** |
| **YouTube** | Videos, live streams, stories; **influencers include special links or promo codes in video descriptions** |
| **TikTok** | **Viral challenges and short-form videos**; a powerful tool for **flash sales and exclusive deals**; influencers use trending audio and effects. *Lecture example: a tech influencer's unboxing video with a **limited-time discount code*** |
| **Instagram** | **Highly visual and interactive**, so very effective for product promotion; the **"swipe-up"** feature in Stories sends users to deals; **Instagram Shopping** turns it into a **full e-commerce hub**: users **browse and buy directly from influencer posts or stories** |

## 3. Social commerce (lecture)
- Platforms **integrate in-app shopping features**.
- Businesses can **promote and sell products directly to consumers within the same platforms where they socialise and consume content**.
- The **real-time nature** of social media lets brands **respond swiftly to trends**, **engage in conversations** and **develop closer relationships** with their audiences.

**E-commerce vs social commerce:**
| E-commerce (website) | Social commerce |
|---|---|
| Customer visits the shop's website | The shop comes to where the customer already is |
| Discovery through search or ads | Discovery through feeds, friends and influencers |
| Checkout on the website | Checkout **inside the app** |
