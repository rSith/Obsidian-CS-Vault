# 11.08 · Content Marketing (and Promoting a Website or Blog)

> [!info] [[11.00 Social Media and Web Influence|Lesson 11]] · Lecture ref L11.8 · Priority 🟡 Medium
> **Used for:** "How do you promote this website among potential customers?" (2019/20 Q5(b)(iii), 5 marks); "you published several blogs but got few comments; how do you publicise them?" (2017/18 Q8(c)).

## 1. What is content marketing?
**Content marketing** means **creating and sharing valuable content** (articles, videos, guides) to **attract and keep an audience**, instead of only advertising directly.

> [!note]
> This definition comes from the study guide. The slides go straight to elements, types and challenges.

## 2. Elements (lecture)
| Element | Lecture explanation |
|---|---|
| **Content creation** | Where the **collateral that engages the audience** is created |
| **Content distribution** | Once created, ensure the content **reaches the target audience** |
| **Content engagement** | **Builds relationships** and provides **valuable feedback** that guides future content strategy |

```text
Create ──► Distribute ──► Engage ──(feedback)──► Create better content …
```

## 3. Types (lecture)
**Blog posts · Video content · Social media content · Podcasting · Infographics · Email marketing · White papers**

## 4. Challenges (lecture)
1. **Creating engaging content**
2. **Maintaining consistency** (posting regularly)
3. **Measuring return on investment (ROI)**

## 5. Promoting a website or blog: combining the whole lesson
| Approach | Techniques |
|---|---|
| **On-page SEO** | Keyword-rich titles, meta descriptions, alt text, headings, simple URLs ([[11.06 On-Page SEO]]) |
| **Off-page SEO** | Backlinks (guest posts, commenting on related blogs, partner sites), XML sitemap ([[11.07 Off-Page SEO]]) |
| **Social media** | Pages, groups, sharing buttons, targeted ads (micro-targeting) ([[11.02 Benefits of Social Media Integration]]) |
| **Influencers** | Micro/nano influencers in the niche; reviews and unboxing; affiliate codes ([[11.03 Influencer Marketing]]) |
| **Content marketing** | Consistent, valuable posts; videos; email newsletters; seasonal campaigns |
| **Web 2.0 tools** | RSS "subscribe" feed, social bookmarking ([[01.07 RSS Feeds]], [[01.08 Social Bookmarking]]) |
| **Engagement** | Ask questions to invite comments; **reply to every comment**; run contests |
