# 12.05 · Challenges (and Solutions) of Ethical Web Development

> [!info] [[12.00 Web Design Ethics and Policies|Lesson 12]] · Lecture ref L12.5 · Priority 🟢 Low

The slide is titled "Challenges and Solutions" but lists only the **challenges**. The solutions below come from the study guide and the rest of the module.

| Challenge (lecture) | Meaning | Possible solutions |
|---|---|---|
| **Balancing functionality with privacy** | Ensuring **user privacy while maintaining the functionality and user experience** | **Privacy by design** ([[10.10 GDPR]]); collect minimal data; anonymise or pseudonymise |
| **Staying updated with compliance regulations** | Keeping up with **constantly changing data-protection laws and compliance requirements** | **Privacy management platforms** (OneTrust, TrustArc); regular audits; staff training |
| **Resource constraints** | Limited **time, budget and technical expertise** to implement ethical practices | Prioritise **high-risk areas**; use existing frameworks, libraries and tools (e.g. accessible components, Bootstrap) |

## Example
A start-up wants personalised recommendations (functionality) but doesn't want to track users excessively (privacy). It could use **on-site behaviour only** (no third-party tracking), **pseudonymised IDs**, and an **opt-in** for personalisation.
