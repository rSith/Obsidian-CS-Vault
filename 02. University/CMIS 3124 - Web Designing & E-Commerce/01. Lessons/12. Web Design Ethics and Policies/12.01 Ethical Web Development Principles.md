# 12.01 · Ethical Web Development Principles

> [!info] [[12.00 Web Design Ethics and Policies|Lesson 12]] · Lecture ref L12.1 · Priority 🟡 Medium (use it as the opening sentence of 2023/24 Q7(d))

## 1. What is ethical web development? (lecture)
- The **"cornerstone of building trustworthy and respectful online environments."**
- An **approach to designing, creating and maintaining web applications and websites that prioritises the rights, safety and well-being of users.**
- It involves a **commitment to transparency, accountability and fairness** in **every aspect** of the web development process.

## 2. The three core principles

> [!note]
> The lecture names the three principles. The explanations and examples are added.

| Principle | Meaning | Web example |
|---|---|---|
| **Transparency** | Be open about what the site does with users and their data | A clear privacy policy; showing the full price up front |
| **Accountability** | Take responsibility for the effects of your design and for protecting data | Reporting breaches; GDPR accountability ([[10.10 GDPR]]) |
| **Fairness** | Treat all users equally and honestly; don't exploit them | No dark patterns; accessible to everyone; unbiased algorithms |

## 3. Why it matters
Users increasingly share personal data and money online. Unethical design (hidden fees, data misuse, inaccessible pages) harms users **and** eventually the business: lost trust, legal penalties, bad reputation. See [[12.03 Impact on Users and Businesses]].
