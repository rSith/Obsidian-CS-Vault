## What this lesson is about
**Ethical web development** puts **users' rights, safety and wellbeing first**. The lesson lists:
1. the **principles** and **five ethical practices**;
2. their **impact on users and on businesses**;
3. **tools and technologies** that support them, and the **challenges**;
4. the **12 dark patterns** that trick users;
5. **web compliance**, and **copyright and intellectual property**.

## Topic structure
```text
Web Design Ethics & Policies
├── Principles: transparency · accountability · fairness
├── 5 ethical practices: privacy · transparency & consent · accessibility & inclusivity · sustainability · security
├── Impact: on users (trust, better UX) · on businesses (reputation, compliance, loyalty)
├── Tools: privacy platforms (OneTrust, TrustArc) · HTTPS/TLS · AES · anonymisation/pseudonymisation
├── Challenges: functionality vs privacy · changing regulations · resource constraints
├── 12 dark patterns
├── Web compliance (legal, data security, accessibility, industry standards) + why it matters
└── Copyright & intellectual property
```
