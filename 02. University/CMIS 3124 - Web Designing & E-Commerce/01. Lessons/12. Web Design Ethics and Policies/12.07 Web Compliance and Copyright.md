# 12.07 · Web Compliance, Copyright and Intellectual Property

> [!info] [[12.00 Web Design Ethics and Policies|Lesson 12]] · Lecture ref L12.7–L12.8 · Priority 🟡 Medium (supports "details to include / not include" on a store; not asked directly)

## Part A: Web compliance

### 1. Definition (lecture)
**Web compliance** is **"the process of ensuring that a website adheres to legal requirements, industry standards and accessibility guidelines."**

### 2. Key aspects (lecture)
| Aspect | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal compliance** | Adhering to laws that govern online activities, such as **data-privacy laws** | **GDPR** (EU), **CCPA** (California) |
| **Data security** | Measures to **protect user information from unauthorised access or breaches** | Encryption, access control ([[13.10 Best Practices for Securing a Website]]) |
| **Web accessibility** | Usable by **everyone, including people with disabilities** | **WCAG**, the **Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)** ([[09.02 Components and Accessibility Guidelines]]) |
| **Industry standards** | Best practices and standards for specific industries | **PCI** compliance for payment-card data ([[10.11 PCI DSS and Consumer Rights]]); **HIPAA** for healthcare websites |

**HIPAA** = Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (US), which protects patients' health information.

### 3. Why is web compliance important? (lecture)
| Reason | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Legal protection** | Avoid fines and lawsuits |
| **User protection** | Users' data and rights are safeguarded |
| **Enhanced user experience** | Accessible, secure, trustworthy sites are better for everyone |
| **Reputation management** | Breaches and lawsuits damage a brand |

## Part B: Copyright and intellectual property laws (lecture)

1. **Do not use copyrighted images, videos or text without permission.**
   - *"Even if you credit the source, using copyrighted material without the owner's consent can result in legal issues."*
2. **Use royalty-free or licensed content**, but **always check the licensing terms**.
3. **If you allow user-generated content, have a policy in place.** If users can upload text, images or videos, your **terms of service** must explain **who owns the content** and **who is responsible if copyright infringement occurs**.

**Key terms:**
- **Copyright:** the legal right of a creator to control copying and use of their work.
- **Intellectual property (IP):** creations of the mind (copyright, trademarks, patents) ([[10.09 E-Commerce Ethics and Ethical Principles]]).
- **Royalty-free:** pay once (or nothing) and use without paying per use, **within the licence terms**.

> [!warning] Common mistake
> "I gave credit, so it's fine" is **wrong**. Credit is not permission.
