# 12.03 · Impact of Ethical Practices on Users and Businesses

> [!info] [[12.00 Web Design Ethics and Policies|Lesson 12]] · Lecture ref L12.3 · Priority 🔴 High (the second half of 2023/24 Q7(d))

## 1. Impact on users (lecture)
| Impact | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Trust and confidence** | Ethical practices **build trust between users and digital platforms**. When users feel **their data is treated with respect and their privacy is protected**, they are **more likely to engage** with the site or app. |
| **Better user experience** | Ethical practices lead to **more user-friendly designs, improved accessibility and overall better experiences**, making platforms **more welcoming and easier to navigate for a diverse audience**. |

## 2. Impact on businesses (lecture)
| Impact | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Enhanced brand reputation** | Known as a trustworthy, responsible brand |
| **Legal compliance and risk mitigation** | Helps businesses **comply with increasingly stringent data-protection laws** (GDPR, CCPA), **reducing the risk of legal penalties and reputational damage** |
| **Long-term customer loyalty** | Users who trust a site return and recommend it |

## 3. How practices lead to impacts (study-guide diagram)
```text
Privacy ─────────────┐
Transparency/consent ┤            ┌─► USERS: trust & confidence → engage more
Accessibility ───────┼──► ethical ┤          better, accessible UX
Sustainability ──────┤   website  └─► BUSINESSES: brand reputation
Security ────────────┘                           legal compliance, fewer penalties
                                                 long-term loyalty
```

## 4. Model answer for 2023/24 Q7(d) (6 marks)
- **Definition (about 1):** ethical web development prioritises users' rights, safety and well-being through transparency, accountability and fairness.
- **Practices (about 3):** respect for privacy (secure, lawful data handling) · transparency and explicit consent · accessibility and inclusivity · sustainability (low energy and carbon) · robust security.
- **Impact on users (about 1):** trust and confidence → more engagement; better, more accessible and inclusive experience.
- **Impact on businesses (about 1):** stronger brand reputation; legal compliance (GDPR, CCPA) and lower risk of penalties; long-term customer loyalty.
- *Depth:* mention a supporting tool (HTTPS/TLS, a consent-management platform).
