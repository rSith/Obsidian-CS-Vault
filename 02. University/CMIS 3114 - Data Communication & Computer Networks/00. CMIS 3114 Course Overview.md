## Important protocols, algorithms and formulas
- **Protocols:** [[3114 Important Protocols]]: HTTP, DNS, SMTP, TCP, UDP, IP, MPLS, Ethernet 802.3, Token Ring 802.5, WiFi 802.11, Bluetooth 802.15, WiMAX 802.16, GSM.
- **Algorithms/procedures:** [[3114 Important Algorithms]]: framing, parity, checksum, CRC, Hamming, sliding window, Go-Back-N, CDMA, ALOHA, CSMA/CD, binary countdown, MACA, store-and-forward, sink tree, Dijkstra, flooding, distance vector, subnetting, service primitives, handoff.
- **Formulas:** [[3114 Important Formulas]]: $2B\log_2V$, $B\log_2(1+S/N)$, dB, $m+r+1\le2^r$, $d+1$ / $2d+1$, $2^{32-n}$, $32-\log_2(\text{block})$, $n(n-1)/2$.
