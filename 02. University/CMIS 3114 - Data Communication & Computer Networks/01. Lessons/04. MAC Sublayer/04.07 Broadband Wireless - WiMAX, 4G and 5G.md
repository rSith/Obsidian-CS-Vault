# 04.07 · Broadband Wireless: 802.16 WiMAX, and WiFi vs WiMAX vs 4G vs 5G

> [!info] [[04.00 MAC Sublayer|Lesson 4]] · Lecture ref Ch4 slide 32 · Priority 🟢 Low (1/6)
> **Asked as:** short note "Wi-Fi Vs WIMAX Vs 4G" (2020/21 Q8b(i)). The slide itself ends with the task *"Compare between WiFi, 4G LTE and 5G."*

## 1. Lecture content
- The problem: *"running fiber or coax to millions of homes and businesses is **prohibitively expensive**"* (the last-mile problem).
- **802.16 = WiMAX** (Worldwide Interoperability for Microwave Access).
- Supports **non-line-of-sight** links using **OFDM** at frequencies **between 2 GHz and 10 GHz**.
- **Competes with mobile 3G and 4G LTE.**

## 2. Comparison table

> [!note] Additional explanation (beyond the slides)
> The lecture names the comparison but gives no table; the figures below are typical published values.

| Feature | **Wi-Fi** (802.11) | **WiMAX** (802.16) | **4G LTE** | **5G** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Network type | **WLAN** | **WMAN** / fixed & mobile broadband | Cellular **WWAN** | Cellular WWAN |
| Range | ~30–100 m | Several km (up to ~50 km fixed) | Several km per cell | 100 m – few km (smaller cells at mmWave) |
| Typical speed | 11 Mbps (b) → 54 (a/g) → 600 Mbps (n), Gbps (ac/ax) | Tens of Mbps | ~100 Mbps (peak higher) | **~1 Gbps+**, low latency (lecture: "1 Gbps, low latency than LTE") |
| Spectrum | Unlicensed **ISM 2.4/5 GHz** | Licensed (2–10 GHz in lecture) | Licensed operator bands | Licensed, incl. mmWave |
| Modulation | OFDM, MIMO (802.11a/g/n) | **OFDM** | OFDMA, MIMO | OFDM, massive MIMO |
| Mobility | Low (walking within a building) | Fixed and some mobility | **High** (vehicles, handoff) | High |
| Owner | User/organisation | Operator / ISP | Mobile operator | Mobile operator |
| Typical use | Home/office LAN, hotspots | Last-mile broadband, rural access | Mobile Internet, voice over LTE | Mobile broadband, IoT, low-latency apps |
