# 02.99 · Lesson Summary: Physical Layer

> [!abstract] Back to [[02.00 Physical Layer]] · Practice: [[PP 02.1 - Transmission Media and Satellites|PP 02.1]] · [[PP 02.2 - Modulation and Data Rate Calculations|PP 02.2]] · [[PP 02.3 - Multiplexing and CDMA|PP 02.3]] · [[PP 02.4 - Switching, Telephone and Mobile Networks|PP 02.4]]

## Must know
- **Guided media:** magnetic media · **twisted pair** (two insulated copper wires twisted to cancel interference; UTP/STP; LANs, phone lines) · **coax** (copper core, insulation, braided shield, jacket; cable TV) · **fibre** (glass core + lower-index cladding + jacket; **total internal reflection**; LED vs laser; backbones, undersea).
- **Wireless:** radio (VLF/LF/MF follow earth; HF bounces off ionosphere) · microwave (line of sight, towers) · infrared (remote controls) · lightwave (lasers; convection currents) · **ISM bands** (unlicensed) · TRC regulates spectrum in Sri Lanka.
- **Satellites:** GEO 35,800 km (3 for coverage, ~270 ms) · MEO (GPS 20,200 km) · LEO (Iridium 66 sats relay in space; Globalstar relays on ground) · VSAT + hub.
- **PSTN:** local loops (analog twisted pair) · trunks (digital fibre) · switching offices; LATA, LEC, IXC, POP; modems/codecs; ADSL (DMT, asymmetric); LMDS.
- **Digital modulation:** baseband (NRZ: +V=1, −V=0; NRZI: 1 = transition; USB) vs passband (ASK, FSK, BPSK, QPSK 2 bits, QAM-16 4 bits, QAM-64 6 bits).
- **Multiplexing:** FDM (bands + guard bands; AM radio; 3100 Hz in 4000 Hz) · OFDM (no guard bands, orthogonal subcarriers; WiFi, 4G) · WDM (wavelengths on fibre) · TDM (time slots, guard time; **T1 = 24 × 64 kbps → 1.544 Mbps**) · CDMA (chip sequences).
- **Nyquist** (noiseless) and **Shannon** (noisy) limits.
- **Switching:** circuit (dedicated path; telephone) vs packet (store-and-forward; Internet) vs message.
- **Mobile:** 1G analog (IMTS, AMPS: cells, frequency reuse, MSC) · handoff (~300 ms) · 2G digital (D-AMPS TDM, GSM: SIM, BSC, MSC, VLR, HLR; 124 channels × 8 slots) · 2.5G EDGE/GPRS · 3G WCDMA/UMTS, CDMA2000 · 4G LTE, WiMAX · 5G ~1 Gbps.
- **Cable TV:** CATV → HFC (fibre nodes); cable Internet is a **shared** medium unlike ADSL.

## Important formulas
$$
\text{Nyquist: } R_{max} = 2B\log_2 V \qquad
\text{Shannon: } C = B\log_2\!\left(1+\tfrac{S}{N}\right) \qquad
\text{dB} = 10\log_{10}\tfrac{S}{N}
$$
$$
\text{CDMA: } S\cdot C = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} S_iC_i \;\Rightarrow\; +1 = \text{bit 1},\ -1 = \text{bit 0},\ 0 = \text{silent}
$$
$$
\text{T1: } 24 \times 64\text{ kbps} + \text{framing} = 1.544\text{ Mbps}
$$

## Important definitions
| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Digital modulation | Converting between bits and the analog signals that represent them |
| Baseband | Signal occupies frequencies from 0 to a maximum; common for wires |
| Passband | Carrier's amplitude/phase/frequency is regulated; signal sits in a band around the carrier |
| Bandwidth | Width of the frequency range transmitted without being strongly attenuated |
| Guard band | Spare frequency between FDM channels to keep them apart |
| Chip sequence | A station's unique m-chip code in CDMA |
| Handoff | Moving a call to a new base station when the signal fades (~300 ms) |
| HFC | Fibre for long-haul runs + coax to the houses |

## Important comparisons
| Twisted pair | Coax | Fibre |
|---|---|---|
| Cheapest, ~100 m, noisy | Shielded, cable TV | Highest bandwidth, EMI-immune, long distance |

| FDM | TDM | OFDM | WDM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency bands + guard bands | Time slots + guard time | Orthogonal subcarriers, no guard bands | Wavelengths on fibre |

| Circuit switching | Packet switching |
|---|---|
| Setup, dedicated path, fixed bandwidth, in order, switch crash fatal, per-minute charge | No setup, store-and-forward, dynamic bandwidth, may reorder, robust, per-packet charge |

| Baseband | Passband |
|---|---|
| NRZ, NRZI; wires | ASK, FSK, PSK, QAM; wireless/optical |

## Important processes
- **NRZI drawing:** start at a level; for each bit, **1 → flip, 0 → stay**.
- **CDMA recovery:** convert codes to ±1 → sum the transmitted (negated for 0) sequences → S·C/m.
- **Handoff:** signal fades → old BS asks neighbours for power readings → MSC/BSC picks strongest, assigns new channel → phone switches (~300 ms).
- **Landline → mobile (GSM):** PSTN → MSC → HLR lookup → serving MSC/VLR → BSC pages mobile → channel allocated.
