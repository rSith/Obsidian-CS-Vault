## Quick revision
- Goals: **confidentiality, integrity, availability** (+ authentication, non-repudiation).
- Threats: eavesdropping, spoofing, malware, phishing, DoS/DDoS, identity theft.
- Tools: **symmetric** (AES) vs **asymmetric** (RSA) encryption, digital signatures, **firewalls**, **proxy servers**, VPNs, IDS, WPA2/WPA3, HTTPS/TLS.
