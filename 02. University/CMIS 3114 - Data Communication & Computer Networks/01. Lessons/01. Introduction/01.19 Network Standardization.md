# 01.19 · Network Standardization

> [!info] [[01.00 Introduction|Lesson 1]] · Lecture ref Ch1 §1.6 (slides 181–182) · Priority 🟢 Low (not asked directly; IEEE numbers are useful everywhere)

## 1. Why standards?
*"Networks have more standards than any other area of the computer industry."* Because *"hardware/software from different manufacturers must all play together – and you can't do that unless the vendors get together and establish agreements about the behavior of this equipment."* Standards are the agreed **protocols** of [[01.10 Protocols and Layering]].

## 2. Standards bodies (lecture list)

| Body | Full name | Known for (additional explanation) |
|---|---|---|
| **ISO** | International Standards Organization | OSI reference model |
| **ANSI** | American National Standards Institute | US member of ISO |
| **NIST** | National Institute of Standards and Technology | US government standards (e.g. security, AES) |
| **IEEE** | Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers | **802 LAN/MAN standards** |
| **IAB** | Internet Architecture Board | Oversees Internet standards (RFCs via the IETF) |
