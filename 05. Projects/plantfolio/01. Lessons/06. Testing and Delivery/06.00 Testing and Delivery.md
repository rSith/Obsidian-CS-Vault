## What this lesson is about
The features are built. Two weeks remain to **prove they work**, **fix what does not**, and **hand the project in**: a tagged release, a report, slides and a demo. Milestone MS-5 defines "ready": a security review done, journeys A–D passing, a usability test held, and **no open Critical or High issues**.

Most of the theory for this stage is already in the vault: testing, usability, accessibility, performance and report writing all have notes. This lesson adds three things specific to the project.

## The report can start early
The roadmap notes that two report sections need only the proposal and wireframes: **DEP-09** (introduction, problem, requirements) and **DEP-10** (design: UX, ERD, architecture). Drafting them during stage F relieves these final weeks. The weekly progress notes in this vault are raw material for the implementation and testing sections: each records what was built, why, and what went wrong.

## What the examiners will try
The same things the roadmap tells you to try on every wired module:
- empty forms and a wrong password;
- oversized and wrong-type files;
- `<script>` in text fields;
- `' OR '1'='1` in search;
- another user's ID in the address;
- the site with the ML service stopped;
- the site at 375 px.

Each has a rule in `CLAUDE.md`, a note in this vault and, by now, a test case.
