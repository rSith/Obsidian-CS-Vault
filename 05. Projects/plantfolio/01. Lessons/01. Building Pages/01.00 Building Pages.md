## What this lesson is about
Twelve of the fifteen pages are built in these three weeks, each matching its wireframe and reading from mock data. No JavaScript yet, and no database. The work on every page is the same four steps:

1. **Frame it:** the shared header, navigation and footer.
2. **Lay it out:** grids of cards, images that keep their shape, bars that stay in view.
3. **Feed it:** read `?id=` or `?q=` from the address and pick the right mock rows.
4. **Cover its states:** what does the page show when the garden is empty, or when a visitor is looking at someone else's plant?

Most of the HTML and CSS theory is already in your CMIS 3124 notes. This lesson adds only what those notes do not cover.

## A checklist for each page
- [ ] Starts with the page pattern: helpers, mock data, `$page_title`, header
- [ ] PHP logic at the top, HTML below
- [ ] Uses the shared components and partials; no new colours or sizes
- [ ] Every printed value goes through `e()`
- [ ] Images have alt text; inputs have labels; one `<h1>`
- [ ] Compared side by side with its wireframe page
- [ ] Checked at 375, 768 and 1024 px: no horizontal scroll, no console errors
- [ ] Its empty and visitor states are built, not only the full one
