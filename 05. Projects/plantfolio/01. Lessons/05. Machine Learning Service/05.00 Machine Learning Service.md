## What this lesson is about
PlantFolio's two extensions beyond the coursework brief:
- **Plant Identifier** (P07): the top three species from a photo.
- **Health Check** (P08): a likely leaf disease from a photo, with a confidence level and an action plan.

Both are **helpers**. Below 60 % confidence they say "Not sure", and nothing is saved without the user pressing Save.

```text
Browser ──photo──▶ PHP page ──saves and resizes──▶ public/uploads/
                      │
                      ├──cURL, multipart, 10 s timeout──▶ Flask ML service (127.0.0.1:5000)
                      │                                      └─ Keras model, loaded once
                      │◀──────── JSON: top 3 with confidence ─┘
                      ├── decides the word: High / Medium / Not sure
                      ├── stores the result in ml_predictions
                      └── shows it; the user decides whether to use it
```
The browser **never** talks to Flask. If Flask is offline, the page falls back to manual entry. The roadmap's rule stands above everything in this stage: **core features before ML, always.**

## The targets (Gate 3)
| Feature | Plan | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Disease detection | PlantVillage, 70/15/15 split; a baseline CNN from scratch, then fine-tuned MobileNetV2 with augmentation | ≥ 90 % test accuracy, **and** measured on 20–30 real phone photos |
| Plant recognition | Fine-tuned MobileNetV2 on the species in `database/species.csv`, **or** the Pl@ntNet API. Decided in week 11 (ML-07). | The correct species in the top 3 for most of ≥ 30 labelled phone photos |
| The service | Offline fallback verified | The site works with Flask stopped |

> [!warning] Know the scope before the viva
> PlantVillage contains **food crops** (tomato, potato, pepper, apple, grape, corn and others) photographed as **single leaves on plain backgrounds**. A houseplant, or a leaf photographed on a balcony, is outside what the model has seen. The Health Check page must list the supported crops and show a scope message for anything else, not a guess.
