# 01.05 · Dates and Epidemiological Weeks

> [!info] [[01.00 Data Collection|Lesson 1]] · Roadmap Phase 1 · Priority 🔴 Core
> **Builds on:** [[Pandas]]
> **Used for:** joining **daily** weather to **weekly** case counts without being one week out.

## 1. Definition
> [!note] Epidemiological week
> An **epidemiological week** (epi week) is a fixed seven-day period used for reporting disease, numbered from 1 to 52 or 53 within a year. Everyone reporting to the same system uses the same start day, so counts can be compared.

## 2. Why it matters
Cases are reported **per week**. Weather arrives **per day**. To put them in one table you must add up the weather for exactly the same seven days the case count covers. If your weeks start on a different day, every weather feature is shifted, and every lag you measure is slightly wrong.

## 3. How it works: week systems differ
| System | Week runs | Week 1 is |
|---|---|---|
| **ISO 8601** (pandas default for `isocalendar`) | Monday – Sunday | The week containing the year's first Thursday |
| **US CDC (MMWR)** | Sunday – Saturday | The first week with at least four days in the new year |
| **Sri Lanka WER** | Check the date range printed on each issue. Recent issues run **Saturday – Friday** (for example 15–21 August 2026). | As numbered in the report |

> [!important] The safe rule
> Store the **start date of the week** (`week_start`) in every table, and join on that. Keep `year` and `week` only as labels. A date is never ambiguous; "week 1 of 2025" is.

## 4. Syntax
```python
import pandas as pd

df["date"] = pd.to_datetime(df["date"])           # text → real dates
df["date"].dt.day_name()                          # Monday, Tuesday, ...
iso = df["date"].dt.isocalendar()                 # columns: year, week, day (ISO, Monday start)

# Weekly bins that END on Friday = Saturday-to-Friday weeks
weekly = df.set_index("date").resample("W-FRI").sum()
```

| Resample rule | Week ends on | Week covers |
|---|---|---|
| `"W"` or `"W-SUN"` | Sunday | Monday – Sunday |
| `"W-SAT"` | Saturday | Sunday – Saturday |
| `"W-FRI"` | Friday | Saturday – Friday |

## 5. Example: daily weather → Saturday-to-Friday weeks
```python
weekly = (
    daily.set_index("date")
         .groupby("district")
         .resample("W-FRI")
         .agg(rain_mm=("rain_mm", "sum"),         # rainfall: ADD the days
              temp_mean=("temp_mean", "mean"),    # temperature: AVERAGE the days
              humidity=("humidity", "mean"),
              days=("rain_mm", "count"))          # how many days went into the week
         .reset_index()
         .rename(columns={"date": "week_end"})
)
weekly["week_start"] = weekly["week_end"] - pd.Timedelta(days=6)
weekly = weekly[weekly["days"] == 7]              # drop incomplete first and last weeks
```

| What | How to combine seven days | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rainfall | **Sum** | Total water that fell |
| Temperature, humidity | **Mean** | A typical level for the week |
| Maximum temperature | **Max** or mean of daily maxima | Depends on the question |

## 6. Week 53 and the turn of the year
- Some years have **53 weeks**. A feature built on "week ÷ 52" breaks there.
- A week can **cross the year boundary**. In the ISO system, 30 December 2024 belongs to week 1 of **2025**. `dt.year` and `dt.isocalendar().year` then disagree.
- For seasonal features, use the **day of the year** of `week_start` instead of the week number: [[03.04 Cyclical and One-Hot Encoding]].

## 7. Common mistakes
- **Assuming the reports use Monday–Sunday.** Check the dates printed on a report.
- **Joining on `(year, week)` from two different week systems.** Rows match, but they describe different days.
- **Averaging rainfall.** Rain is summed.
- **Keeping incomplete weeks.** The first and last bins may hold only two or three days.
- **Sorting dates stored as text.** `"10/01/2024"` sorts before `"2/01/2024"`. Convert first.
- **Day-month confusion.** `01/02/2024` may be 1 February or 2 January. Pass `format=` or `dayfirst=True`.
- **Ignoring time zones.** A "day" in UTC is not the same 24 hours as a day in Colombo. Ask the API for local time.

## 8. Exam and viva focus
- **Define** epidemiological week, and say why a shared definition is needed.
- **ISO week rule:** Monday start; week 1 contains the first Thursday.
- **Why store `week_start`?** A date is unambiguous; a week number depends on the system.
- **Aggregation:** sum for totals such as rainfall, mean for levels such as temperature.
- **Why can a year have 53 weeks?** 365 days is 52 weeks plus one day (two in a leap year), and the extra days add up.
