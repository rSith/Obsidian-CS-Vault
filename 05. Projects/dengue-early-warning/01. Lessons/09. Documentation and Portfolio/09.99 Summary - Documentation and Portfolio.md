# 09.99 · Lesson Summary: Documentation and Portfolio

> [!abstract] Back to [[09.00 Documentation and Portfolio]] · Progress: [[Phase 09 - Documentation and Portfolio]]

## Must know
- **Goal:** a stranger understands what was built, why it works and where it stops working, in five minutes.
- **IMRaD:** Introduction, Methods, Results and Discussion, plus abstract, related work, data, limitations, conclusion, references.
- **Write in this order:** figures → results → methods → discussion and limitations → introduction → abstract.
- **Every claim needs:** the number, the unit, the comparison, and where to see the evidence.
- **State limitations plainly:** reporting delay, under-reporting, weather at one point per district, unusual years, what weather cannot explain, few outbreaks in the test period.
- **Architecture diagram:** one box per component, labelled arrows showing data flow, one level of detail, and a walk-through paragraph beside it.
- **Three tiers:** dashboard (presentation), Flask (logic), database (data). Plus the scheduled pipeline and the monitoring.
- **Story structure:** problem → approach → one striking result → what it means and where it fails.
- **STAR** for interviews: situation, task, action, result.
- **Show a failure.** It makes the successes believable.
- **Responsible AI:** safe, fair, transparent, accountable, with people in control.
- **WHO's six principles:** autonomy; well-being, safety and public interest; transparency and explainability; responsibility and accountability; inclusiveness and equity; responsive and sustainable.
- **The data measures reporting as well as disease.** Weakly reporting districts can look safest.
- **Decision support, not decision making.** Say so on the dashboard itself.

|---|---|

| Decision support | Decision making |
|---|---|

| Weak claim | Strong claim |
|---|---|
