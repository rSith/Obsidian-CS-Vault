## What this lesson is about
A forecast of "262 cases" is not yet useful to a health officer. Is 262 a lot for that district in that week? This lesson turns numbers into decisions:

1. **Measure the forecast** in case units: how far off is it?
2. **Define "normal"** for every district and week of the year, from history. That band is the **endemic channel**.
3. **Turn a forecast into an alert:** Normal, Watch or Warning.
4. **Measure the alerts:** how many real outbreaks were caught, how many alarms were false, and how early.
5. **Show the uncertainty** and **present it clearly**.

Then the **final test** is run, once, on years the model has never seen.

## The rules of the final test
1. Every choice (model, features, thresholds) is made on **validation** data.
2. The test period is run **once**.
3. Whatever it shows is reported, including the outbreaks that were missed.
