# 02 · Cleaning and Exploratory Analysis

## What this lesson is about
Phase 1 left you with raw files. This lesson turns them into **one clean table with one row per district per week**, and then studies that table to answer the question the whole project depends on: **how many weeks after the weather do the cases follow?**

1. **Build** the weekly table by merging cases, weather and population.
2. **Check** its quality: gaps, duplicates, impossible values, unusual periods.
3. **Explore** it: seasonal shape, trend, how strongly each week resembles the last.
4. **Measure the lag** between weather and cases, per district.
