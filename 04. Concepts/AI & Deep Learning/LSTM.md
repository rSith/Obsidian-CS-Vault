# LSTM
> LSTMs (Long Short-Term Memory networks) are ==a special kind of artificial neural network designed to process sequential data==. Unlike regular neural networks that treat each piece of data independently, LSTMs remember past information over long periods. They solve the "forgetting" problem that older recurrent networks ([[RNN]]s) had.
