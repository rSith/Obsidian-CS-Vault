## [[Artificial Intelligence]]
> The broadest concept. AI refers to systems designed to **perform tasks that typically require human-like intelligence**.

- Understanding language
- Recognizing images
- Making decisions
- Planning
---
## [[Machine Learning]]
> A **way of building AI systems** that learn patterns from data instead of relying entirely on explicitly programmed rules.

- Instead of programming, we give the model thousand of houses with their characteristics and prices. It learns the relationship itself.

>[!important]
>Machine Learning is **one way to build AI**. Not all AI is Machine Learning.

---
## [[Neural Network]]
> A Neural Network is a **type of Machine-Learning model** inspired loosely by how biological neurons work.

- It consists of interconnected computational units called **neurons**

$$Input→[Neurons]→[Neueons]→Output$$

useful for:
- Image/Speech recognition
- Prediction
- Classification
- Natural Language Processing

---
## [[Deep Learning]]
> Deep Learning uses neural networks with multiple layers. The word deep refers ainly to having many layers.

- A traditional/simple neural network may have fewer layers, while deep neural networks can have many layers.
$$Input→Layer 1→Layer 2→Layer 3→Layer 4→ Output$$
use for:
- Computer Vision
- Speech recognition
- Large language Models([[LLMs]])
- Image generation
