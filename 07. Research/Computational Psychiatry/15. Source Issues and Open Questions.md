# Source Issues and Open Questions

Back to [[00. Computational Psychiatry - Research Home|Research Home]] · Source: [[01. Paper - What is Computational Psychiatry (Antonucci 2026)|Antonucci 2026]]

These are places where the paper is **inconsistent, unclear, or too thin to rely on alone**. None of them changes the paper's main message, but knowing them helps if you cite the paper in your project.

## D. Open questions worth thinking about for an FYP

> [!info] My questions, prompted by the paper's own stated challenges (not claims from the paper)
> 1. **Generalisability** (p. 9): could a model trained in one population, e.g. European university centres, work in Sri Lanka? What would need to be re-validated?
> 2. **Theory + data together** (p. 3): can theory-driven parameters (e.g. α, β from a reward task) be used as *interpretable features* in an ML model? This would combine both strategies and answer the black-box concern.
> 3. **XAI** (p. 10): which explanation methods do clinicians actually find useful?
> 4. **Digital phenotyping** (pp. 3, 9): could smartphone or online-behaviour data stand in for lab tasks, as in the paper's negativity-bias example?
> 5. **Ethics** (p. 10): how should data privacy, algorithmic bias and over-reliance be handled in a student project that uses mental-health data?
