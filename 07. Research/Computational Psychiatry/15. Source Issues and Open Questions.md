# Unclear Points in the Paper

Back to [[00. Computational Psychiatry - Research Home|Home]]

These don't change the main message, but are worth knowing before you cite the paper.

## 1. Small errors
| Where | Issue |
|---|---|
| DSM / ICD references | The text says ICD-11, but the reference list gives ICD-10 |
| p. 3 | Says "U-shaped" but means an **upside-down U** (best in the middle) |
| p. 7, learning formula | The text gets the order of the prediction error backwards and calls the formula a "product". The figure's version, $V + \alpha(R - V)$, is correct |
| p. 7 | "Interval models" should be "internal models" |
| Figure 2C | The Bayes formula is missing its bottom part (the denominator) |

## 2. Weak citations
- The claim about AI choosing medication from genes cites a study on **Parkinson's disease**, not psychiatry.
- Relapse-detection apps and AI therapy apps have **no citation**.

*(Judged from reference titles only.)*

## 3. Details the paper leaves out
- The equations for the panic disorder model.
- The full formula of the PRT learning model, and any PRT results.
- Whether "rewarded 3 times more" means more often or bigger rewards.
- How symptom networks are calculated.
- Which XAI methods to use.

## 4. Questions for my project *(my own, not from the paper)*
1. Would a model trained in Europe work for patients in Sri Lanka?
2. Can theory-based numbers (like α and β) be used as features in an ML model, so it is more explainable?
3. Could phone or online data replace lab tasks?
