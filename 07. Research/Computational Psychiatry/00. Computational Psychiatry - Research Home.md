# Computational Psychiatry: Research Home

Simple-English notes on one paper: **Antonucci et al. (2026), "What is Computational Psychiatry? An introductory narrative review"**, *Journal of Psychopathology*.

## In one paragraph
Computational Psychiatry (CP) uses maths and computers to understand mental disorders. It has two approaches. **Theory-driven** research starts with an idea and turns it into a mathematical model. **Data-driven** research starts with large datasets and uses machine learning to find patterns. Together they aim to make diagnosis and treatment more precise and personal.
