# Symptom Networks

Back to [[00. Computational Psychiatry - Research Home|Home]] · Previous: [[10. Data-driven Modelling - AI and Machine Learning]] · Source: paper p. 8

## 1. The idea
Symptoms are seen as **parts that interact with each other**, not just signs of one hidden disease.

| Traditional view | Network view |
|---|---|
| One hidden disorder causes all the symptoms | Symptoms affect and maintain each other |

**Example** *(not from the paper)*: poor sleep → tiredness → poor concentration → worry → poor sleep.

## 2. How it looks
- **Nodes** (circles) = symptoms
- **Lines** = links between symptoms
- **Line thickness and colour** = strength and direction of the link

This is the same as a **graph** in computer science.

## 3. Why it is useful
It helps explain:
- why patients with the same diagnosis can be different,
- why symptoms go up and down over time.

## 4. Related example
The panic disorder model in [[04. What is a Model]] works the same way: symptoms reinforce each other in loops.

> The paper doesn't explain how these networks are calculated from data.
