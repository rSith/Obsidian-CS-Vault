> [!abstract] What it is about
> Compare cloud, hybrid and fully local AI coding setups and their cost/speed/privacy tradeoffs.

> [!important] What I'll learn
> - How each workflow choice shifts cost, speed and data exposure
> - **Afterwards I can:** Choose and configure a coding-agent workflow that fits your budget and privacy needs
> - **Main output:** Same Python app rebuilt in cloud, hybrid and local workflows with usage comparison
>
> **Covers:** Layers of a coding setup (agent, model, where it runs, work split); subagents with focused context; cheaper models for routine work; OpenCode with OpenRouter; local models via LM Studio; spec-driven rebuilds
> **Tech:** Claude Code, Haiku, OpenCode, OpenRouter, LM Studio, Python, terminal
