> [!abstract] What it is about
> Serve open-source LLMs efficiently with quantization and vLLM, then benchmark the deployment.

> [!important] What I'll learn
> - The optimize-deploy-benchmark workflow and its accuracy/speed/cost tradeoffs
> - **Afterwards I can:** Quantize, serve and benchmark an open model for many concurrent users
