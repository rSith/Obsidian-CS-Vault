> [!abstract] What it is about
> Implement linear quantization from scratch and build a general PyTorch quantizer, including 2-bit weight packing.

> [!important] What I'll learn
> - The mechanics and error tradeoffs of linear quantization
> - **Afterwards I can:** Write and apply your own quantizer to open models
> - **Main output:** General-purpose 8-bit quantizer and 2-bit packing implementation
>
> **Covers:** Quantize/dequantize; scale and zero point; symmetric vs asymmetric; per-tensor, per-channel, per-group; weights and activations; custom 8-bit quantizer replacing PyTorch layers; quantizing any open model; loading from HF Hub; packing/unpacking 2-bit weights; beyond linear quantization
> **Tech:** Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Hub
