> [!abstract] What it is about
> Introduction to model compression: data types, downcasting and linear quantization with Hugging Face and Quanto.

> [!important] What I'll learn
> - Why and how quantization shrinks models

> **Tech:** Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, Quanto
