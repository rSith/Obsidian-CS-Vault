> [!abstract] What it is about
> Post-train LLMs with SFT, DPO and online RL (PPO/GRPO) and know when to use each.

> [!important] What I'll learn
> - The three main post-training methods and their data needs
> - **Afterwards I can:** Choose and run a post-training method to change model behaviour
> - **Main output:** SFT instruct model, DPO identity-changed model, GRPO math-improved model
>
> **Covers:** Pre- vs post-training; SFT pipeline (base to instruct); DPO with chosen/rejected pairs (change assistant identity); online RL (PPO, GRPO) and reward design; GRPO with verifiable math reward; data curation principles
> **Tech:** Python, Hugging Face models/TRL-style pipelines, PyTorch

## Before I start
- **Stated prerequisites:** Familiar with LLM basics
- **What I actually need:** PyTorch/HF basics; fine-tuning concepts
