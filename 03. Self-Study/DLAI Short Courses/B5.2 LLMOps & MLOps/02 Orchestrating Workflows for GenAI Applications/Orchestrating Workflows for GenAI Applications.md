> [!abstract] What it is about
> Turn a notebook RAG prototype into scheduled, resilient Airflow 3.0 pipelines.

> [!important] What I'll learn
> - Orchestration practices for GenAI workloads
> - **Afterwards I can:** Automate and harden a GenAI data/inference pipeline
