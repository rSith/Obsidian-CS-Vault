> [!abstract] What it is about
> Turn images, audio and video into LLM-ready, queryable text and build a multimodal RAG system.

> [!important] What I'll learn
> - Pipelines that convert unstructured media into searchable data
> - **Afterwards I can:** Build a cross-modal RAG pipeline over meeting audio, slides and video
> - **Main output:** Multimodal RAG over meeting recordings with grounded, cited answers
>
> **Covers:** Multimodal data challenges; OCR and ASR to text; unified vector space for cross-modal search; prompting VLMs; frame sampling and embedding for video; timestamped segment descriptions; multimodal RAG with citations
> **Tech:** Python, SQL, Snowflake, OCR, ASR, Vision Language Models, embeddings
