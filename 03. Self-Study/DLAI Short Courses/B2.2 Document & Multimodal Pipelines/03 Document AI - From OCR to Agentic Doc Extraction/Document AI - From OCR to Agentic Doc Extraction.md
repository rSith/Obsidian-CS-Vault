> [!abstract] What it is about
> Extract structured data from complex documents with agentic, vision-based parsing and deploy RAG on AWS.

> [!important] What I'll learn
> - Agentic document understanding and production document pipelines
> - **Afterwards I can:** Build a document-extraction-to-RAG pipeline that runs serverlessly
> - **Main output:** Research-paper chatbot on AWS with ADE preprocessing
>
> **Covers:** Traditional OCR and its limits; OCR history; PaddleOCR; layout detection and reading order; VLMs for documents; LandingAI ADE (Markdown/JSON, visual grounding, schema extraction); ADE-powered RAG with image crops; event-driven AWS pipeline (S3, Bedrock Knowledge Base, Strands Agents)
> **Tech:** Python, OCR, PaddleOCR, layout/reading-order models, VLMs, LandingAI ADE, vector DB, AWS S3, Bedrock, Strands Agents
