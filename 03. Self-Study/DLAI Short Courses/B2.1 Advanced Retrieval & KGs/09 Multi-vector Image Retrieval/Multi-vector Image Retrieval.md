> [!abstract] What it is about
> Retrieve images and visual documents accurately with multi-vector (patch-level) embeddings.

> [!important] What I'll learn
> - Late-interaction retrieval and how to make it memory-efficient
> - **Afterwards I can:** Build fine-grained visual-document search and RAG
> - **Main output:** Multimodal RAG over course materials with ColPali
