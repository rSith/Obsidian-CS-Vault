> [!abstract] What it is about
> Learn JAX by building and training a 20M-parameter GPT-style model from scratch.

> [!important] What I'll learn
> - End-to-end LLM build-train-checkpoint-infer workflow in JAX
> - **Afterwards I can:** Implement and train a small transformer LM in JAX
> - **Main output:** Trained MiniGPT (20M params) with chat interface
>
> **Covers:** JAX primitives (autodiff, JIT, vmap) vs NumPy/PyTorch/TensorFlow; MiniGPT architecture with Flax/NNX (embeddings, transformer blocks); tokenization and batching with Grain; training loop with Optax; checkpointing with Orbax; inference via chat UI
> **Tech:** Python, JAX, Flax/NNX, Grain, Optax, Orbax
